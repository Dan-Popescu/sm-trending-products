## U01: A readable product decision dossier

- Present the existing decision brief in simple French: measured support, contrary
  evidence, missing information and useful next checks. Explain navigation states
  without calling a product a winner or authorizing launch.
- Show meaningful original quantities, units, bounds, assumptions, selected country,
  cutoff and exact dossier revision. Unknown values remain unknown. Do not invent
  a time series from aggregate windows or combine ad reach with video views.
- Keep technical codes and provenance inspectable in secondary details, not the
  primary reading path. Unknown future codes have an honest readable fallback.
- Supply safe bounded human-readable CLI/export output through the same pure
  presentation interface. Preserve default raw JSON and analytical identifiers.
  Escape exported HTML and redact current secrets, URLs and source-derived text
  before presentation. Never alter an executable plan to make it exportable.
- Refresh source guards on action, drop derived reports on every interaction and
  retain no source quotes in session state. No analysis runs merely on navigation.

## Architecture and verification

### Verified continued frontier

An independent native/Streamlit audit reproduced the following additional work.
These are authorized correctness/usability fixes and a measured optimization,
not new platform or deployment scope:

- **U05, source-scoped creator identity:** use one validated source/kind/value
  identity in scoring, trend windows and diagnostics. Two creators from different
  sources with the same raw ID must not collapse; malformed metadata cannot become
  a known creator through string conversion. Invalidate computed feature caches
  with a new version while retaining raw-window reuse. Old immutable published and
  frozen records remain unchanged; corrected new results have their own content
  hashes. Valid unaffected cases retain parity, and cold/warm/freeze paths agree.
- **U06, bounded guarded text hydration:** the audit measured 61 SQL executions
  for one completed text and 327 for eight, including unused analysis-state queries
  and repeated scope/provenance checks. Consolidate page selection and hydration
  behind one authoritative bounded guarded operation. Complete content reports and
  identities must remain identical; aggregation does not fetch unused analysis
  state, while selection still honors completed, retired, running and uncertain
  analysis semantics. Measure the actual improvement and preserve every source,
  namespace, ownership, availability, retirement and cursor boundary.
- **U07, truthful supplied-text support:** existing brief support incorrectly says
  quoted mentions are available whenever eligible texts exist, including no-mention
  and unsupported-language texts. Report actual candidate/negated/ambiguous mentions
  with their distinct meaning; availability or unsupported text alone cannot become
  positive theme or buyer-demand evidence. No absence-of-demand claim is inferred.
- **U08, shortlist across catalog pages:** selecting products on one 200-row page
  currently loses them on the next page. Keep at most ten validated canonical IDs
  across pages, with explicit removal and workspace isolation. Persist only bounded
  operator-selection IDs, never source reports or quotations; preview/application
  revalidates exact namespace, current product/evidence access and plan scope.
